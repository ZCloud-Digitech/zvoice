## 1. Privacy Policy

**Landing page: YES — High Priority**

Footer link:

`Privacy Policy`

Should explain how Z Call Agent collects, uses, stores, protects, shares, and deletes data.

For a voice-agent SaaS, cover as applicable:

- Account information
- Business/customer information
- Phone numbers
- Call metadata
- Voice/audio recordings
- Transcripts
- AI-generated summaries
- Appointment information
- IP/device/log information
- Cookies and analytics
- Third-party providers
- Data retention/deletion
- User rights
- International transfers where applicable

---

**Landing page: YES — High Priority**

`Terms of Service`

- Account creation

## 4. Cookie Preferences

**Landing page: YES**

Footer link:

`Cookie Preferences`

This should open the actual cookie preference/consent UI, not merely a static page.

Example:

```text
Necessary Cookies       Always Active
Analytics Cookies      On / Off
Functional Cookies     On / Off
Marketing Cookies      On / Off
```

Categories must match the technologies actually implemented.

- Proration

- Non-refundable charges
- Prepaid credits/minutes
- Processing time
- Refund request process

## 7. Disclaimer

**Landing page: YES — Recommended**

Footer link:

`Disclaimer`

Especially important for an AI voice product.

Should explain:

- AI agents can make mistakes
- AI-generated information may require verification
- Z Call Agent does not guarantee perfect responses
- Businesses are responsible for agent configuration
- Customers are responsible for applicable laws
- Automated calling must be used lawfully
- AI/human disclosure requirements may apply
- The platform is not automatically a substitute for professional advice

---

## 8. Acceptable Use Policy

**Landing page: YES — Highly Recommended**

Footer link:

`Acceptable Use Policy`

Important because Z Call Agent enables automated voice communication.

Should prohibit/restrict:

- Fraud
- Scams
- Harassment
- Threats
- Illegal activity
- Impersonation
- Unlawful robocalling
- Spam
- Telephony abuse
- Circumventing consent requirements
- Malicious automated calls
- Unauthorized data collection
- Violations of applicable telecom/privacy/consumer laws

Include suspension/termination rights for abuse.

# Signup Legal Links

The landing page should not be the only place where legal documents are linked.

At signup, provide relevant acceptance/acknowledgement:

```text
By creating an account, you agree to the Terms of Service
and acknowledge the Privacy Policy.

[ ] I agree to the Terms of Service
```

If marketing consent is required:

```text
[ ] I want to receive product updates and marketing communications.
```

Keep optional marketing consent separate from mandatory contractual acceptance where required.

## Legal Implementation Note

This document defines the **landing-page information architecture and placement** of policies, not jurisdiction-specific legal advice.

Final policy text should be reviewed for the jurisdictions where Z Call Agent and its customers operate. Automated voice calls, call recording, transcripts, consent, marketing communications, personal-data processing, international transfers, and AI disclosure can create additional requirements depending on the use case and location.

Having a policy page does not by itself establish legal compliance.
